# 🇸🇪 Swedish Employment Law

Managers in Sweden need a working map of employment protection, work environment, discrimination,
collective agreements, and loyalty duties — then they involve HR/legal for real cases. This page
is orientation for engineering leaders, **not legal advice**.

**Start here:** L&E Global overview + LAS (Wikipedia) for the statutory spine, Arbetsmiljöverket
for work environment duties, DO for discrimination. Use union/legal firm notes when a live
situation appears.

## Core employment framework

📄 [Employment and labour law overview — Sweden](https://leglobal.law/countries/sweden/employment-law/employment-law-overview-sweden/)
by L&E Global  
Practical summary of contracts, working time, leave, and collective bargaining context.

📄 [Employment Protection Act (LAS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Employment_Protection_Act_(of_Sweden))
by Wikipedia  
Core rules on employment contracts, termination, and notice — the statute managers hear about
most often.

📄 [Changes to the Swedish Employment Protection Act](https://www.vinge.se/en/news/changes-to-the-swedish-employment-protection-act-and-other-labour-laws/)
by Vinge  
Overview of recent LAS and related labour-law updates (fixed-term contracts, termination
procedures). Confirm current state with counsel — reforms move.

📄 [Employment & Labour Laws — Sweden](https://iclg.com/practice-areas/employment-and-labour-laws-and-regulations/sweden)
by ICLG  
Structured country report covering hiring, contracts, and terminations.

📄 [Collective agreement coverage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collective_agreement_coverage)
by Wikipedia  
Why collective agreements matter as much as statute in the Swedish labour market.

## Work environment and discrimination

📄 [Swedish Work Environment Authority](https://www.av.se/en/)  
Official English entry point for the Work Environment Act, employer responsibilities, and safety
rules.

📄 [Acts and regulations about work environment](https://www.av.se/en/work-environment-work-and-inspections/acts-and-regulations-about-work-environment/)
by Arbetsmiljöverket  
Primary regulations employers must prevent ill health and accidents under.

📄 [Equality Ombudsman (DO) — English](https://www.do.se/choose-language/english)  
Employer obligations under the Discrimination Act and active measures to promote equality.
