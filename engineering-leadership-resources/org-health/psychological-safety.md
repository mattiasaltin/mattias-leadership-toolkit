# 🛡️ Psychological Safety

Psychological safety is the belief that the team is safe for interpersonal risk — speaking up,
asking for help, admitting mistakes. It is a performance condition for learning organisations,
not “being nice.”

**Start here:** Edmondson (*Fearless Organization* + talk), then Clark’s four stages when you
need a practical maturity language. Treat Sinek/Brown as complementary framing, not substitutes
for the research.

## Resources

📘 [The Fearless Organization](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/41817482-the-fearless-organization)
by Amy C. Edmondson  
The seminal book: what psychological safety is, why it drives learning and performance, and how
leaders build it deliberately.

🎥 [Creating Psychological Safety at Work in a Knowledge
Economy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KUo1QwVcCv0) by Amy Edmondson  
A concise summary of why safety drives innovation and what leaders can do to make speaking up
normal.

🎥 [The Four Stages of Psychological Safety](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hc68qSppJio) by
Timothy Clark  
Inclusion → learner → contributor → challenger safety — a practical ladder for teams.

🎥 [How Psychological Safety Has Been the Bedrock of Key Innovation Programs at
Google](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-zrLI-Osg88)  
Google’s experience connecting psychological safety to collaboration and innovation (Project
Aristotle context).

🎥 [A Lesson on the Psychology of Meetings from SNL and Google](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_NqdKdafRfk)
by Charles Duhigg  
How meeting norms reveal (and reshape) psychological safety.

🎥 [Why Good Leaders Make You Feel Safe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lmyZMtPVodo) by Simon
Sinek  
Complementary “circle of safety” framing on trust — not a substitute for Edmondson.

🎥 [The Power of Vulnerability](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iCvmsMzlF7o) by Brené Brown  
Vulnerability and authenticity as trust ingredients — adjacent to psychological safety.

📄 [Leaders, Stop Trying to Be Heroes](https://hbr.org/2021/10/leaders-stop-trying-to-be-heroes)
by Hortense le Gentil  
Humility and limits as leadership strengths that make room for the team.

📄 [How to Silence Your Change Agents in 3 Simple
Steps](https://mattiasaltin.substack.com/p/how-to-silence-your-change-agents) by Mattias Altin  
Author note: common ways organisations unintentionally suppress challenge and new ideas.
