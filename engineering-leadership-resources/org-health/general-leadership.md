# 📚 General Leadership

General leadership is the craft of setting direction, building trust, and adapting your style as
scope grows — from first-line EM to leading leaders. Prefer a few spine books over a pile of
motivational talks.

**Start here:** *The Manager's Path* for the EM career arc, Lena Reinhard for BICEPS in engineering
context, *An Elegant Puzzle* when org design and systems show up.

## Resources

📘 [The Manager's Path](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/33369254-the-manager-s-path) by
Camille Fournier  
Stages of tech leadership from mentoring to managing managers — still the clearest EM career map.

📘 [An Elegant Puzzle](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/45303387-an-elegant-puzzle) by Will
Larson  
Systems of engineering management: team sizing, org design, and managerial leverage.

📘 [First, Break All the Rules](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/50937.First_Break_All_the_Rules)
by Marcus Buckingham and Curt Coffman  
Gallup-based research on what great managers do differently — strength-focused engagement over
generic “best practices.”

🎥 [What Engineering Managers Should Do (And Why We Don't)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q_bJVokYLRI)
by Lena Reinhard  
BICEPS needs in an engineering-manager context. See also
[Key Frameworks Referenced](../../README.md#key-frameworks-referenced).

🎥 [Challenges of a New Engineering Manager](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BJBtLwCkMuY) by
Thomas Bartel  
Practical first-time EM mistakes and how to avoid them.

📘 [Staff Engineer](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/56481725-staff-engineer) by Will Larson  
Leadership beyond the management track — useful when you lead through influence, not headcount.

📘 [Think Again](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/55539565-think-again) by Adam Grant  
Rethinking assumptions and teaching others to do the same — a leadership habit under change.

🎥 [What it takes to be a great leader](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aUYSDEYdmzw) by Roselinde
Torres  
Three questions for 21st-century leadership: anticipate change, diversify your network, abandon
outdated success habits.

📄 [Ten Fatal Flaws That Derail Leaders](https://hbr.org/2009/06/ten-fatal-flaws-that-derail-leaders)
by Jack Zenger and Joseph Folkman  
Common derailers (micromanagement, weak feedback, neglected development) and how to interrupt them.

🎥 [Why Middle Management is the Hardest Job](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C0W4H6jMLKg) by
Simon Sinek  
The squeeze between senior leadership and the team — empathy without losing accountability.

📄 [Engineering Management Resources](https://github.com/charlax/engineering-management)  
A durable curated list of EM learning materials (complement to this toolkit, not a substitute).
