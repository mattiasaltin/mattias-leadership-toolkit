# 💬 Conflict & Difficult Conversations

Conflict is normal in healthy teams. Leadership skill is turning disagreement into clearer
decisions without eroding trust. This page covers frameworks for hard conversations, feedback
under pressure, and repairing ruptures.

📘 [Crucial Conversations: Tools for Talking When Stakes Are
High](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/15014.Crucial_Conversations) by Kerry Patterson, Joseph
Grenny, Ron McMillan, and Al Switzler  
A practical toolkit for high-stakes dialogue: staying in dialogue, making it safe, and moving to
action when emotions run hot.

📘 [Radical Candor: Be a Kick-Ass Boss Without Losing Your
Humanity](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/29906992-radical-candor) by Kim Scott  
How to challenge directly while caring personally — the stance most difficult conversations need.
Also listed under coaching; here the focus is conflict and feedback under tension.

📘 [Nonviolent Communication: A Language of
Life](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/71730.Nonviolent_Communication) by Marshall B. Rosenberg  
A structured way to separate observation, feeling, need, and request — useful when conflict is
stuck in blame.

🎥 [The Secret to Giving Great Feedback](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wtl5UrrgU8c) by LeeAnn
Renninger  
Short, concrete feedback techniques that reduce defensiveness.

📄 [How to Handle a Disagreement on Your
Team](https://hbr.org/2017/07/how-to-handle-a-disagreement-on-your-team) via Harvard Business
Review  
Practical guidance for surfacing disagreement productively without escalating too early.
