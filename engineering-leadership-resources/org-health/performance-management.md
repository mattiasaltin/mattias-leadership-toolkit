# 📋 Performance Management

Performance management is how leaders set expectations, give ongoing feedback, run fair reviews,
and handle underperformance without surprises. Done well it is continuous coaching; done poorly
it becomes annual theatre.

📘 [High Output Management](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/324750.High_Output_Management) by
Andrew S. Grove  
Still the clearest engineering-leadership classic on task-relevant maturity, one-on-ones, and
managing output through others.

📘 [Radical Candor: Be a Kick-Ass Boss Without Losing Your
Humanity](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/29906992-radical-candor) by Kim Scott  
A usable model for continuous feedback so reviews are never the first time someone hears the
truth.

📘 [The Manager's Path: A Guide for Tech Leaders Navigating Growth and
Change](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/33369254-the-manager-s-path) by Camille Fournier  
Practical guidance on feedback, growth conversations, and what changes as you manage managers.

📄 [OKRs](https://www.whatmatters.com/faqs/okr-meaning-definition-example) by whatmatters.com
(John Doerr / Measure What Matters)  
A clear primer on Objectives and Key Results for tying performance conversations to outcomes.
For product-side OKR practice see
[🎯 Objectives](../../product-leadership-resources/objectives.md).

📘 [An Elegant Puzzle: Systems of Engineering
Management](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/45303387-an-elegant-puzzle) by Will Larson  
Systems thinking for org design, team sizing, and the managerial systems that make performance
management scale.
