# 🎯 Coaching & Mentoring

Coaching and mentoring are how leaders develop talent day to day. This page is the canonical home
for shared coaching practice used by both engineering and product leaders.

**Start here:** Whitmore (GROW), *The Coaching Habit* for short questions, Radical Candor for
feedback stance. Product-specific coaching lives on
[🎓 Coaching](../../product-leadership-resources/coaching.md).

## Resources

📘 [Coaching for Performance](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/225159108-coaching-for-performance)
by John Whitmore  
Source text for the GROW model (Goal, Reality, Options, Way forward). See also
[Key Frameworks Referenced](../../README.md#key-frameworks-referenced).

📘 [The Coaching Habit](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/29342515-the-coaching-habit) by
Michael Bungay Stanier  
Seven short questions that keep ownership with the coachee.

📘 [Radical Candor](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/29906992-radical-candor) by Kim Scott  
Care personally, challenge directly — the feedback stance coaching needs. Canonical listing;
conflict and performance link here instead of re-listing.

📘 [Trillion Dollar Coach](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/42118073-trillion-dollar-coach) by
Eric Schmidt, Jonathan Rosenberg, and Alan Eagle  
Bill Campbell’s playbook for coaching senior leaders in tech.

📄 [The Coaching Mindset](https://www.svpg.com/the-coaching-mindset/) by Chris Jones  
Mindset shifts for coaching growth and accountability (also used on the product coaching page).

🎥 [Are You Their Coach, Mentor, Counselor or Consultant?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e7V5JGRrQ6g)
by Valorie Burton  
Pick the right stance instead of defaulting to advice-giving.

🎥 [Coaching with the GROW Model](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XbkXpdiyNs0) by ProjectManager  
Short visual GROW primer before reading Whitmore.

🎥 [The Secret to Giving Great Feedback](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wtl5UrrgU8c) by LeeAnn
Renninger  
Concrete techniques for feedback people can use (also under conflict).

🎥 [The Art of Listening](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qpnNsSyDw-g) by Simon Sinek  
Why listening underpins coaching relationships.
