# 🔑 Empowerment

Empowerment is giving people control matched to competence and clarity — not abandoning them to
“figure it out.” Leaders design intent, language, and growth systems so ownership is real.

**Start here:** Marquet (*Turn the Ship Around!*) for intent-based leadership, then EMPOWERED for
product-team empowerment context. Cleese for the creative conditions autonomy needs.

## Resources

📘 [Turn the Ship Around!](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/16158601-turn-the-ship-around) by
David Marquet  
Intent-based leadership: distribute control with competence and clarity — the empowerment
classic.

📘 [Leadership Is Language](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/42774083-leadership-is-language)
by David Marquet  
How language enables or blocks problem-solving and ownership.

🎥 [Leadership Is Language](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AUTYxGKbp5w) by David Marquet  
Talk companion: shifting from command-and-control language to collaborative intent.

🎥 [Leaders Tune Control to Competence and Clarity](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J5jQsrMousE)
by David Marquet  
The empowerment formula: control scales with competence + clarity.

🎥 [Autonomy Doesn't Mean "Do Whatever"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RiKN7x1ppRE) by Jimmy
Janlén  
Autonomy with alignment and mutual accountability — not chaos.

📘 [EMPOWERED](https://www.svpg.com/books/empowered-ordinary-people-extraordinary-products/) by
Marty Cagan  
Product-team empowerment in the EMPOWERED operating model. See
[Key Frameworks Referenced](../../README.md#key-frameworks-referenced); coaching detail on
[🎓 Coaching](../../product-leadership-resources/coaching.md).

📄 [Creativity in Management](https://jamesclear.com/great-speeches/creativity-in-management-by-john-cleese)
by John Cleese (via James Clear)  
Open-mode conditions (space, time, confidence, humour) that make empowered creative work
possible.

📄 [What Having a Growth Mindset Actually Means](https://hbr.org/2016/01/what-having-a-growth-mindset-actually-means)
by Carol Dweck  
Growth mindset as a team cultural ingredient for ownership and resilience.

🎥 [How to Take Great Engineers & Make Them Great Technical
Leaders](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RtMmxqkPVug) by Courtney Hemphill  
Empowering ICs into technical leadership without erasing their strengths.
