# 🔧 Infrastructure

Infrastructure is the product that delivers your product: environments, platforms, reliability,
and the operating model around them. Leaders decide what is self-service, what is shared, and
how incidents and change are handled without heroics.

**Start here:** Google SRE for production mindset, Morris for infrastructure-as-code practice,
Team Topologies when platform-vs-stream team boundaries are unclear.

## Resources

📘 [Site Reliability Engineering](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/) by Google  
How to run production systems at scale: error budgets, toil reduction, monitoring, and incident
response as engineered work.

📄 [The Site Reliability Workbook](https://sre.google/workbook/table-of-contents/) by Google  
Companion practices and case studies for implementing SRE ideas outside Google’s context.

📘 [Infrastructure as Code](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/26544394-infrastructure-as-code)
by Kief Morris  
Principles and patterns for managing infrastructure with the same discipline as application code.

🎥 [Infrastructure as Code, Patterns and Practices](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K843Ukiw3d8)
by Kief Morris  
Practical IaC patterns grounded in real operating experience.

📄 [Team Topologies](https://teamtopologies.com/) by Matthew Skelton and Manuel Pais  
Platform, stream-aligned, enabling, and complicated-subsystem teams — the org design lens for
infrastructure ownership and cognitive load.

📄 [AWS Well-Architected Framework](https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/well-architected/)  
A structured review lens (reliability, security, cost, performance, operations) even if you are
not all-in on AWS.

📄 [CNCF Landscape](https://landscape.cncf.io/)  
Map of the cloud-native ecosystem — useful for orientation, dangerous as a shopping list.
