# ✅ Testing

Testing is how teams keep the cost of change low. Leaders own the strategy (pyramid,
environment, feedback speed) and the culture that treats tests as product code — not a phase
after “real” work.

**Start here:** Fowler’s test pyramid for shape, Feathers when the codebase is legacy, Beck when
you want TDD as a design practice rather than a coverage ritual.

## Resources

📄 [The Test Pyramid](https://martinfowler.com/articles/practical-test-pyramid.html) by Martin
Fowler  
How to balance unit, service, and UI tests for speed and confidence — the default leadership
model for test investment.

📘 [Test-Driven Development: By Example](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/387190.Test_Driven_Development)
by Kent Beck  
Foundational TDD: tests as a design tool that enables confident refactoring. Also listed under
technical excellence; here the focus is team testing strategy.

📘 [Working Effectively with Legacy
Code](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/44919.Working_Effectively_with_Legacy_Code) by Michael
Feathers  
Characterisation tests, seams, and safe paths when you inherit systems with little automation.

📘 [Growing Object-Oriented Software, Guided by
Tests](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/4268826-growing-object-oriented-software-guided-by-tests)
by Steve Freeman and Nat Pryce  
Outside-in TDD and how tests drive better architecture over time.

📘 [The Art of Unit Testing](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/209113300-the-art-of-unit-testing-third-edition)
by Roy Osherove  
Practical patterns for readable, maintainable unit tests that teams will keep.

🎥 [Are Your Tests Slowing You Down? • Trisha Gee • GOTO
2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e0gwyyO3Gd8) by Trisha Gee  
When suites become a bottleneck — and how to keep quality without killing flow.

🎥 [Testing in Production](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b2oota_FhGY) by Charity Majors  
Why production is part of the verification story, and what leaders must put in place before
“test in prod” is responsible.

📄 [Behaviour-Driven Development (BDD)](https://cucumber.io/docs/bdd/) by Cucumber  
Shared language for behaviour when product and engineering need examples that stay executable.
