# 🔢 Product Priorities

Product priorities focus teams on outcomes rather than output. Effective prioritisation balances
customer value, business impact, and strategy — and resists scoring every idea while avoiding
hard choices.

OKRs express organisational focus once priorities are chosen; see
[🎯 Objectives](objectives.md). This page is about *what to work on* and *what to defer*.

**Start here:** *Escaping the Build Trap* for outcome focus, Shape Up for an operating
alternative to endless backlogs.

## Resources

📘 [Escaping the Build Trap](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/42611408-escaping-the-build-trap)
by Melissa Perri  
How to prioritise outcomes over feature factories — the clearest book-length treatment of the
build trap.

📘 [Shape Up](https://basecamp.com/shapeup) by Ryan Singer  
Shaped pitches, appetite-based bets, and fixed time / variable scope — prioritisation without a
infinite backlog.

📄 [The RICE Framework for Prioritisation](https://www.intercom.com/blog/rice-simple-prioritization-for-product-managers/)
by Intercom  
Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort as one input — not a substitute for strategy.

🎥 [Annual Planning and the Art of Roadmapping](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_HsnipD3FDA) by
Sachin Rekhi  
Reforge 4D roadmapping: strategy, vision, customer, and business lenses instead of scorecards
alone.

📄 [The Kano Model](https://www.productplan.com/glossary/kano-model/) by ProductPlan  
Basic, performance, and delight needs — so teams do not over-invest in the wrong kind of
satisfaction.

📄 [Product vs Feature Teams](https://www.svpg.com/product-vs-feature-teams/) by Marty Cagan  
Why prioritisation looks different on empowered product teams versus feature factories.

📄 [The Four Big Risks](https://www.svpg.com/four-big-risks/) by Marty Cagan  
Value, usability, feasibility, and viability — a lens for discovery bets, not only delivery
sequencing.
