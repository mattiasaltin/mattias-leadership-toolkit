# 🔍 Product Discovery

Discovery is how product teams reduce the risk of building the wrong thing. It is continuous
learning with customers — not a phase that finishes before delivery starts.

📘 [Continuous Discovery Habits: Discover Products that Create Customer Value and Business
Value](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/58033215-continuous-discovery-habits) by Teresa Torres  
The modern default for continuous discovery: opportunity solution trees, weekly interviews, and
assumption testing inside product trios.

🎥 [Build better products with continuous product discovery | Teresa
Torres](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RFaz9ZBXpk) by Teresa Torres  
A long-form conversation on making discovery a weekly habit while still shipping.

📄 [Dual-Track Agile](https://svpg.com/dual-track-agile/) by Marty Cagan  
How discovery and delivery run together so learning does not block (or get blocked by) shipping.

📘 [The Lean Startup](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/10127019-the-lean-startup) by Eric Ries  
Validated learning, build-measure-learn, and treating product work as experiments — foundational
context for modern discovery.

📄 [Opportunity Solution Trees](https://www.producttalk.org/opportunity-solution-trees/) by
Teresa Torres  
A practical structure for connecting desired outcomes to opportunities and solution ideas.
