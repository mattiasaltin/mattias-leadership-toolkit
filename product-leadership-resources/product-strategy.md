# 🗺️ Product Strategy

Product strategy turns vision into intentional choices: which problems to solve, for whom, and
in what order. It is mostly saying no — not a longer roadmap.

**Start here:** Rumelt for what “strategy” even means, Intercom for trade-offs in practice. For
the vision/strategy boundary, see
[🌟 Product Vision](product-vision.md) (*Vision vs. Strategy*).

## Resources

📘 [Good Strategy Bad Strategy](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/11721966-good-strategy-bad-strategy)
by Richard Rumelt  
Diagnosis, guiding policy, and coherent action — still the best filter for strategy fluff.

📄 [Product strategy best practices](https://www.intercom.com/blog/product-strategy-means-saying-no/)
by Intercom  
Trade-offs and focus: strategy as choosing what not to do so the important work gets done.

📄 [5 Steps to a Winning Product Strategy](https://www.productplan.com/learn/product-strategy)
by ProductPlan  
Practical framing of market focus, differentiation, and sequencing without confusing strategy
with a roadmap dump.

📘 [Jobs to Be Done: Theory to Practice](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/31579659-jobs-to-be-done)
by Anthony Ulwick  
Customer need through JTBD — what people are trying to accomplish, not what features they
request.

📘 [Crossing the Chasm](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/61329.Crossing_the_Chasm) by Geoffrey
A. Moore  
Adoption lifecycle and positioning when moving from early adopters to mainstream markets.

📘 [The Innovator's Dilemma](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/2615.The_Innovator_s_Dilemma) by
Clayton M. Christensen  
How disruption reshapes markets — and why incumbents’ “rational” strategies fail.

📘 [The Lean Startup](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/10127019-the-lean-startup) by Eric Ries  
Build-measure-learn as a strategic learning loop (also foundational for discovery).

📘 [Blue Ocean Strategy](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/4892.Blue_Ocean_Strategy) by W. Chan
Kim and Renée Mauborgne  
Creating uncontested market space when competing head-on is a losing game.
