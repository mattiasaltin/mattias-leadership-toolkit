# 📣 Product Evangelism

Product evangelism is the continuous work of sharing vision and purpose so people become
missionaries for the product — not mercenaries executing a feature list. It is storytelling,
internal alignment, and culture-building.

**Start here:** *Made to Stick* for memorable ideas, Doerr’s missionaries framing for culture,
then product-specific storytelling practice.

## Resources

📘 [Made to Stick](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/69242.Made_to_Stick) by Chip Heath and Dan
Heath  
What makes ideas memorable — the craft behind vision stories that survive retelling.

🎥 [Mercenaries and Missionaries](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ui8yLqCxChM) by John Doerr  
The missionary vs mercenary framing product leaders still use for culture and motivation.

📘 [The Storytelling Edge](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/35448018-the-storytelling-edge) by
Joe Lazauskas and Shane Snow  
Practical storytelling techniques for building product culture internally and externally.

🎥 [Mastering the Art of Storytelling as a PM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwWq6KfsjXU) by
Munawwar Tayob  
Making product narratives land with non-product audiences.

📄 [The Most Important Thing](https://www.svpg.com/the-most-important-thing/) by Marty Cagan  
Why communicating a strong product vision is a primary product leadership responsibility.
