# 🧭 Product Principles

Product principles are the beliefs that shape day-to-day product decisions. Prefer a short,
memorable set teams actually cite in debates — long posters nobody uses are theatre.

**Start here:** Intercom for writing craft, GOV.UK for a public worked example. Keep SVPG
*Principles* for why principles beat technique lists.

## Resources

📄 [How to Write Product Principles That Guide Your
Team](https://www.intercom.com/blog/product-principles/) by Intercom  
Practical advice on principles that are memorable, actionable, and actually influence decisions.

📄 [GOV.UK Design Principles](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/government-design-principles) by GOV.UK  
A clear public set showing how concise statements guide trade-offs across teams and services.

📄 [Amazon's Leadership Principles](https://www.aboutamazon.com/about-us/leadership-principles)
by Amazon  
Principles used at organisational scale — broader than product, still a strong reference for how
principles operate in hiring and decisions.

📄 [Principles](https://www.svpg.com/principles/) by Marty Cagan  
Why principles matter more than techniques in modern product work.
