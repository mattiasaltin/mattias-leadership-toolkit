# 🌟 Product Vision

Product vision is the organisation’s North Star: a shared picture of a future where the product
has changed how people work, live, or solve problems. It is aspirational enough to inspire and
concrete enough to reject feature laundry lists.

**Start here:** Jeff Patton’s “science fiction” framing, then Pichler for writing tips. Use
*Inspired* as the Cagan spine for how strong product orgs treat vision.

## Resources

📄 [Product Vision is Science Fiction](https://jpattonassociates.com/product-vision-is-science-fiction/)
by Jeff Patton  
Imagine a future world where your product exists and tell that story — not revenue targets or a
feature wish list.

📄 [8 Tips for Creating A Compelling Product Vision](https://www.romanpichler.com/blog/tips-for-writing-compelling-product-vision/)
by Roman Pichler  
Practical guidance for a vision that is inspiring, shared, and concrete enough to guide
decisions.

📄 [Vision vs. Strategy](https://www.svpg.com/vision-vs-strategy/) by Marty Cagan  
Canonical home for the vision/strategy split — what belongs in the North Star versus the plan of
attack. Strategy page links here rather than duplicating.

📘 [Inspired: How to Create Tech Products Customers Love](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/35249663-inspired)
by Marty Cagan  
How compelling product vision guides decisions in strong product organisations. Prefer this page
as the canonical *Inspired* listing; other topics link here when they only need the vision
chapters.

📘 [The Lean Product Playbook](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/26098219-the-lean-product-playbook)
by Dan Olsen  
Frameworks for developing vision and validating it through customer feedback and iteration.

🎥 [Tips for Building a Compelling Product Vision](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fW-zCm1Ig3Y) by
Will Najar  
Product School talk on aligning stakeholders around a durable north star.
