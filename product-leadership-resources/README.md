# 🌟 Product Leadership Resources

## 👋 Introduction

Hello, I’m [Mattias](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mattiasaltin/)!

Product leadership is the craft of deciding what is worth building — and building the conditions
so teams can learn and ship without drowning in feature factory noise. This collection is the
product half of the toolkit: vision, strategy, discovery, principles, priorities, evangelism,
and the people systems (staffing, coaching, objectives) that make empowered teams real.

The topic map is inspired by Marty Cagan’s EMPOWERED accountabilities. Treat that as scaffolding
for browse order, not as a single-author syllabus — pages pull from Patton, Intercom, GOV.UK,
SVPG, and others where they are sharper for the job.

Primary formats:

- 📘 Book  
- 🎥 Video/Talk  
- 📄 Article/Blog Post  

Podcasts and research papers appear where they are the best source.

## 📚 Overview

Product leadership has two kinds of accountability:

- **Leadership** — direction and strategy (vision, strategy, discovery, principles, priorities,
  evangelism)
- **Management** — people and execution systems (staffing, coaching, objectives)

Shared coaching and recruiting craft with engineering lives in the engineering org-health pages;
product pages add the product-specific layer and link across. For EMPOWERED vocabulary see
[Key Frameworks Referenced](../README.md#key-frameworks-referenced).

## 🚀 Start Here for New Product Leaders

If you're new to product leadership, here's a recommended path (matches ⬅️/➡️ browse order):

1. **Foundation:**
   - [Product Vision](product-vision.md) - North Star that rejects feature laundry lists
   - [Product Strategy](product-strategy.md) - Sequenced choices about where to play
   - [Product Discovery](product-discovery.md) - Learning what to build before (and while) shipping
   - [Product Principles](product-principles.md) - Decision rules teams actually cite
2. **Leadership accountabilities:**
   - [Product Priorities](product-priorities.md) - Focus on outcomes, not activity
   - [Product Evangelism](product-evangelism.md) - Alignment without theatre
3. **Management accountabilities:**
   - [Staffing](staffing.md) - Building great product teams
   - [Coaching](coaching.md) - Developing product talent
   - [Objectives](objectives.md) - Goals that connect to delivery reality

**How to use these resources:**

- Follow Start Here for a learning path, or jump to a topic when facing a specific challenge
- Prefer canonical homes (*Inspired* on Vision, shared coaching on Engineering Coaching) over
  duplicate listings
